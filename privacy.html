Privacy Policy & Data Notice

1. Data Controller Identity

This website and AI Digital Twin platform are operated by Anees Munir Khokhar ("Data Controller"), based in Islamabad, Pakistan. For any privacy queries, data subject requests, or access requests, contact:

  • Email: aneesmunir1020@gmail.com
  • Website: https://aneesportfolio.com

2. Data We Collect & Processing Purposes

We strictly adhere to data minimization principles. We process only two categories of data:

  • Contact Form Submissions: If you voluntarily send a message via our contact form, we collect your Name, Email Address, and Message Text. This data is used solely to evaluate project opportunities and respond to your message.
  • AI Digital Twin Chat Queries (Anonymized Telemetry): When you chat with our AI twin, your question text and timestamp are logged to measure chat system accuracy. Your IP address is immediately hashed using SHA-256 and never stored in plain text.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

  • UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL): Processing is based on explicit, freely given, affirmative consent obtained via checkbox prior to contact form submission.
  • Pakistan Constitution (Art. 14) & PECA 2016: Processing is based on legitimate interest in managing professional recruiter communications and maintaining cybersecurity.

4. Zero-Cookie Policy

This portfolio is 100% cookie-free. We do not place tracking cookies, advertising beacons, or third-party analytical cookies on your device. No cookie consent popups are required because no cookies are set.

5. Data Retention & Automatic Purge

In accordance with data minimization rules, all chat query logs and analytics records are automatically purged after 90 days. Contact messages are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill professional communication.

6. Your Data Subject Rights

Regardless of your geographic location, you hold the following rights over your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of any personal data stored about you.
  • Right to Deletion (Right to be Forgotten): Request immediate erasure of your contact submission.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate contact details.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for message processing at any time.

To exercise any right, send an email to aneesmunir1020@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Subject Request". Requests are fulfilled free of charge within 30 days.

7. Security Measures

We implement technical measures to prevent unauthorized data access or loss, including SSL/TLS HTTPS encryption, Strict HSTS headers, SHA-256 IP hashing, Content Security Policies (CSP), and automated rate-limiting protections.
